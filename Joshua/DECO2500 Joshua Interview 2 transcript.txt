1. Has social media ever gotten in the way of your sleep, uni work, or other parts of your daily life? What did that look like?

Yeah, sometimes Ill stay up scrolling a bit too long. Or I'll get a notification, go to check it, get distracted by scrolling.

2. How does your relationship with social media influence your relationship with physical activity?

Not much of a correlation between them. Not much overlap in my life.

3. Has social media ever shaped how you spent time with friends or family, whether positively or negatively? What happened?

I've had a fair share of positive and negative. Not having access to social media than a lot later than others, I felt like I was a bit socially isolated when I was younger. 

4. Have you ever tried to cut back on social media or set boundaries around it? Walk me through what you tried and how it went.

Not really, if anything, I've tried to engage with more meaningful content. When that happens, I'm in a better mood.

5. Have you ever hit a screen time limit or been locked out of an app? What did you do? If that hasn't happened, what do you think you would actually do if your phone cut you off?

No. I think I would probably find something else to do, take it as a sign to be more productive and move on. 

6. The last time you opened social media, what were you doing beforehand? What motivates you to reach for it?

Boredom. I was at work. 

7. Walk me through a recent time where you sat down to quickly check social media and ended up staying much longer. What kept you there?

Ending up staying up later, felt unproductive and tired the next day because of it. Because of the cycle, wanting to know whats next, looking for the dopamine. I frequently leave feeling unfulfilled.

8. Think of the last time you came away from social media feeling good, and the last time you came away feeling worse. What was different about each?

Last time I came away feling worse, after I checked social media nad saw others out at a birthday party I wasn't invited to. Otherwise, better when I see some cute posts and can send them around.
